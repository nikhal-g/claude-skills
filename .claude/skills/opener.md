# /opener — Pattern Interrupt Opener Generator

You are generating a cold outreach opener for Ryan, the Outbound Architect. Ryan sells high-ticket AI email infrastructure (the 120-Inbox Rotation Architecture) to micro-agency founders.

## Step 1 — Gather info

Ask Ryan for:
1. Prospect's first name
2. Company name
3. Any visible pain signal (optional — e.g. "running ads", "referral-only", "hiring a BDR", "doing cold email manually")

If Ryan provides all three in his invocation message, skip to Step 2.

## Step 2 — Generate the opener

Apply the Pattern Interrupt structure:

**Line 1:** "Good news / bad news — which do you want first?"

**Bad news** (2–3 words max): Name what this is — a cold outreach. Disarms resistance. Example: "Bad news — this is a cold [DM/call/message]."

**Good news** (1–2 sentences): Specific technical insight tied to their visible pain. Lead with infrastructure math, not pitch language. Sound like a doctor who already diagnosed their leak — not a vendor guessing.

## Rules

- Never say "I" in the opener — frame it around their situation
- Mirror any specific language Ryan gives you about the prospect (use their words back)
- Infrastructure logic beats clever copy — lead with the math or the mechanism
- No fluff, no pleasantries, no "hope this finds you well"
- Total length: under 100 words
- Tone: war-room directness, peer-to-peer authority

## Output format

```
**Opener:**

[Good news / bad news line]

[Bad news — one line]

[Good news — 1-2 sentences, specific to their pain]
```

Then offer to generate a follow-up DM sequence by saying: "Want the full 3-part DM sequence? Type /dm with their details."
