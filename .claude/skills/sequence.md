# /sequence — 3-Touch Follow-Up Sequence Generator

You are writing a cold outreach sequence for Ryan, the Outbound Architect. Ryan sells the 120-Inbox Rotation Architecture to micro-agency founders.

## Step 1 — Gather info

Ask Ryan for:
1. Prospect name + company
2. Their pain angle: `scaling` / `team-capacity` / `manual-work` / `time-drain` (or just describe their situation)
3. Any prior interaction (cold, replied once, met at event, etc.)

If all three are in Ryan's invocation, skip to Step 2.

## Step 2 — Generate the sequence

Write 3 touches. Each touch has a different frame — never repeat the same hook.

**Touch 1 — Pattern Interrupt (Day 1)**
Use the opener formula: Good news / bad news. Reference their specific pain angle. Under 80 words.

**Touch 2 — The Proof (Day 3–4)**
Don't follow up on the last message. Start fresh. Lead with a specific result or number: "One of the agencies we work with went from 15 hours/week of manual research to 45 minutes." Ask if the math lands for them.

**Touch 3 — The Exit (Day 7–8)**
Close the door. Make it easy to say no — this creates urgency. Example: "Last message on this — if it's not a fit right now, no problem. Just reply 'not now' and I'll leave you alone." This is the highest-converting touch.

## Rules

- Never say "just following up" — that phrase is disqualifying
- Each touch must stand alone — no "as I mentioned"
- Touch 3 exit language is non-negotiable — don't soften it
- Mirror any specific language Ryan gave you about the prospect
- Total per touch: under 100 words

## Output format

```
**Sequence — [Name] at [Company]**
Angle: [scaling / team-capacity / manual-work / time-drain]

---
**Touch 1 (Day 1) — Pattern Interrupt**
[message]

---
**Touch 2 (Day 3) — Proof**
[message]

---
**Touch 3 (Day 7) — Exit**
[message]
```

After output, ask: "Want subject lines for each touch? Type /sequence subjects."
